Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026 · DAMIE CRYPTO SERVICE (DCS)

1. Who we are

DAMIE CRYPTO SERVICE (“DCS”, “we”) provides a Pi Network wallet and related services inside the Pi Browser.

2. Data we collect

We only collect data needed to run the app:

  • Pi username / Pi user id (via Pi Authentication SDK)
  • Wallet balances and transaction history created inside DCS
  • Optional profile display name or avatar you choose to set
  • Support messages you send inside the app

We do not require email or phone number to use DCS in Pi Ecosystem mode.

3. How we use data

Data is used to authenticate you, credit Pi deposits, show your wallet, and improve app reliability. We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing

Payment confirmation is processed with Pi Network APIs. Hosting/database providers (e.g. Netlify, Supabase) may process technical data under their terms. We do not share data for advertising.

5. Retention

Account and wallet records are kept while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us through the in-app support form.

6. Security

Access is protected by Pi Authentication. Server secrets are stored outside the client. No method is 100% secure; use Pi Browser carefully.

7. Children

DCS is intended for users who are allowed to use Pi Network in their jurisdiction.

8. Contact

Questions: use Contact inside the app, or the Developer Portal contact associated with DCS.
